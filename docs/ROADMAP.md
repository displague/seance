# From vertical slice to production

The AAA target is an ambition, not the status of this checkout. Validate the social
hook with human players before scaling content or paying for a large asset library.

## Next playable milestone

- Run a 5–6-person headset playtest. Measure Medium airtime, evidence trading,
  ghost inactivity, goal completion, session length, and how often trust changes.
- Replace raw PCM with a tested voice transport/codec and jitter management;
  add device selection, listener mute/volume, and a deliberately opt-in spectator mix.
- Give the Ghost additional actionable memory fragments and nonverbal channels.
- Add negotiated inheritance proposals and goals that influence another guest's action.
- Make possession an invitation/resistance decision with private compulsions.
- Support reconnect and snapshots that preserve a sitting without disclosing secrets.

## Content and art

- Six distinct production characters with clean hands/clothing, facial expression,
  seated/standing transitions, readable silhouettes, and grounded locomotion.
- Modular manor art with correct UVs, authored PBR texture sets, collision/LOD,
  baked or budgeted lighting, and optimized static geometry.
- Original music, spatial manifestations, voiced fragments, subtitle timing,
  and accessible non-audio equivalents for critical clues.
- A second authored case before procedural generation. Reusable case schemas,
  solvability checks, motive-interaction graphs, and explicit balancing budgets.

## Release gates

- Multi-machine LAN/WAN tests, packet loss/latency, host crashes, reconnect, six-client soak.
- Server-owned physics or an explicit trusted-party model; protocol versioning,
  authentication, relay integration, moderation, and abuse controls.
- Rebinding, controller support, UI scaling, localization, contrast, motion and sound options.
- Save/version migration, settings validation, reproducible builds, crash reporting opt-in.
- Minimum-spec and long-session performance; shader/texture budgets and asset cleanup.
- Steamworks integration and release-template exports, signing, licenses/provenance review,
  platform requirements, store materials, and independent QA.

Not in the current build: vengeful temperament selection, revenant compulsions,
See No Evil/Hear No Evil, evidence forging/stealing/hiding, player elimination,
ghost hunters, branching occupation paths, matchmaking, or public spectators.
