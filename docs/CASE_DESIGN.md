# A mystery dinner with something to lose

Information, not a fixed good/evil team assignment, is the game's common currency.
Every guest should have partial testimony, a desired discovery, a protected secret
and a reason to negotiate. A sincere ally on inheritance may lie about a death.
Generated dialogue can be evasive or mistaken; only recovered evidence and validated
actions change authoritative facts, ownership, votes or scores.

## Ashwood's implemented three chapters

| Investigation | Newly available | New pressure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1: The Last Night | Tonic, watch, botanical and relationship clues; keepsakes; candles | Establish accounts, protect secrets, claim valuables |
| 2: Blood and Inheritance | Final will, nursery register, spirit radio | Establish lineage, trade testimony, redirect inheritance; Lenore may give her locket to Iris |
| 3: The Final Audit | Physician's ledger, independent bank audit, reliquary | Challenge concealment, finish preparations, commit to a final story |

The dossier and estate guide disclose this schedule from arrival. Goals do not
change silently. All gates live in rules, including NPC and MCP callers. A sealed
object is inspectable for its summary and opening time, but cannot be collected or
prepared early. Any living character may prepare an unsealed apparatus; a completed
one names its preparer and explains the shared benefit instead of appearing broken.

The bank audit is an independent public reference. Its explicit action enters the
copy into everyone's record; it cannot be pocketed, gifted or destroyed. Destroying
the original ledger cannot silently eliminate a future audit opportunity. Both the
Ghost's financial-proof goal and Blackwell's concealment goal name this alternative
from the outset. This is counterplay, not restoration of destroyed evidence.
The Ghost can ask a willing NPC through a legal listening channel from sitting two
to examine and publish that reference when the final investigation opens. The NPC
must physically reach it; accepting the request neither reveals it immediately nor
bypasses chapter/voice rules. Blackwell refuses this self-incriminating errand.

The register still matters to the final reliquary and heir claim. These dependencies
are stated in the starting dossier and destruction confirmation. Candles plus radio
remain a release path even without the register. Social opponents can still obstruct
announced goals; the design does not guarantee that everyone can score everything.
The nursery itself is not in the current four-room playable wing, and its key is an
inheritance keepsake, not a concealed access prerequisite.

NPCs reset their goal pass each chapter, pursue newly eligible objects, reconsider
known preparation clues and patrol between stations. They state new priorities and
can transfer their own possessions; they never accept a human's trade or spend a
human's property. Asking what a guest has found discloses only what that guest is
willing to offer. A private ownership claim is remembered per listener rather than
being exposed by magically accurate request menus.

## Reuse direction, not a shipped multi-game engine

Ashwood remains one fixed case. `CaseData` separates authored content and
`CaseProgression` separates its chapter schedule, but roles, final-truth conditions,
NPC plans and the manor are still case-specific. Do not advertise arbitrary case
loading, procedural murder mysteries or multiple shipped settings.

The next content milestone should be a second authored story with a genuinely
different conflict—for example a midnight radio broadcast or a vanished passenger
on an ocean liner. Build its case definition from cast, starting knowledge, evidence
dependencies, chapter events, allowed deception, goals, apparatus and endings. Use
that second story to identify the shared code that deserves extraction; do not build
a speculative universal engine first.

Authoring checks should flag unknown late prerequisites, single destructible paths,
goals requiring another player's consent, contradictory testimony and knowledge
leaks. Reuse the isolated party-playtest workflow across successive complete cases,
preserving failures and comparing actual actions with subjective model ratings.
Human mystery-dinner/voice sessions remain necessary to judge tension and enjoyment.
